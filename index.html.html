Sallatha Sutta (The Two Arrows)

When an untrained mind experiences discomfort, it reacts with grief and resistance. It experiences two pains: physical and mental. Like being struck with an arrow, then immediately struck with a second arrow.

Taṇhā (Craving & Aversion)

*Taṇhā* refers to the mental grip or aversion we bring to raw emotions. Suffering doesn't come from feeling sadness or anger, but from desperately trying to push it away or replay its story.

Prajñā (Wisdom Sword)

*Prajñā* is clear-sighted wisdom. It acts like a sharp blade that cuts through delusion and over-identification, leaving the mind open, grounded, and clear.

Anattā (Non-Self)

Emotions are temporary weather patterns passing through awareness. Instead of saying "I am anxious," recognize that "Anxiety is currently present."
