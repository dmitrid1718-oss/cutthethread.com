2. R.A.I.N. Mindfulness Protocol

Step-by-step psychological unhooking method created by Tara Brach for transforming second-arrow suffering.

R Step 01

Recognize

Acknowledge what is present right now. Name the second arrow without judgment.

A Step 02

Allow

Let life be as it is. Pause the impulse to fight, fix, or run away from the sensation.

I Step 03

Investigate

Explore with gentle curiosity: "Where do I feel this in my physical body?"

N Step 04

Nurture

Offer self-compassion. Recognize that you are the awareness, not the story.

R - Recognize What Is Happening

Active Focus

Recognizing means consciously noticing the thoughts, emotions, or physical sensations that are present in this moment. When second-arrow mental drama arises, simply say silently: "Ah, anxiety is here," or "Here is the story that I am not enough."

Prompt: "What mental story or tightness am I experiencing right at this exact second?"
